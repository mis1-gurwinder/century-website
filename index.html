About Us

Century is the consumer brand of Dinero FoodWorks Pvt. Ltd., a food company with roots going back to 1958.

Our story

The yellow line under "ur" in our logo is there on purpose. It stands for a simple belief: you are central to everything we make.

We make everyday foods that are fresh, nature-forward and easy to cook, from Soya and Makhana to Besan, Poha and single-serve sachets.

  1. 1958The family food business, now DFPL, is founded.
  2. 1980The Century brand is born.
  3. TodayIn-house manufacturing in Punjab, supplying retail and food service.
